WHO WE ARE

From community challenges to practical solutions.

ELDEO was established in 2020 to empower youth, women and communities through education, mentorship, sustainable livelihoods, environmental conservation and digital inclusion.

Our story

We were driven by the reality that poverty, limited educational opportunity, unemployment, environmental pressure and digital exclusion often reinforce one another. ELDEO responds by combining local knowledge with practical skills, technology and community action.

Our approach is simple: listen to communities, build capacity, test practical ideas, measure results and scale what works.

“Technology is a voice for expansion and a tool for production.”

Our mission

To leverage local talents and resources to create income-generating opportunities, advocate for an inclusive society, create environmental awareness, promote cultural heritage and drive holistic human development, using technology as a voice for expansion and a tool for production.

Our vision

Resilient and empowered communities where every person has the opportunity to learn, thrive and build a sustainable future.

WHAT WE DO

Programs designed around real community needs.

Our work connects people, knowledge, technology, local resources and opportunities.

🎓

Education, Mentorship & Guidance

Mentorship, counselling, career guidance, life skills and school-community initiatives that help learners remain in education and make informed decisions.

💻

Digital Skills & ICT

Basic computer skills, digital literacy, e-commerce, freelancing, MS Office, graphic design, coding, content creation and digital marketing through community learning spaces.

♻️

Green Enterprise & Livelihoods

Upcycling waste, natural biofertilizers, biochar, biopesticides, handmade products and other circular-economy pathways that can create green jobs.

🌱

Climate & Environmental Action

Tree planting, rainwater harvesting, school gardens, environmental clubs and locally appropriate climate-resilience solutions.

👧🏾

Adolescent Girls & Boys

Life-skills and mentorship addressing school dropout, early pregnancy, early marriage, HIV/AIDS, drug abuse and other risks affecting young people.

🏘️

Community Development

Locally owned initiatives that strengthen community participation, livelihoods, inclusion and resilience in underserved areas.

PROJECTS & INITIATIVES

Ideas turned into community action.

Climate + Education

Harvesting Hope

A rainwater-harvesting and school-garden initiative linking environmental resilience, food production and student wellbeing. The initiative planted 200 trees and supported 200 students.

Adolescent Mentorship

Adolescent Mentorship & Life Skills

Mentorship reaching adolescent girls and vulnerable boys with guidance around education, health, decision-making, rights and positive futures.

Girls + Life Skills

ABSTAIN YOU GAIN CLUB

A school-based initiative launched in 2024 to strengthen life skills, confidence, decision-making and educational continuity for girls.

Women + Youth

ECOGROW Community Hub

A community hub concept bringing together circular-economy skills, environmental action, digital learning and livelihood opportunities.

Digital Economy

Digital Skills & Online Entrepreneurship

Training youth and women in digital marketing, content creation, freelancing and social-media management to improve employability and access to online markets.

Digital Inclusion

Community ICT Hub

A practical space for digital learning, community data work, skills development and access to opportunities in the digital and circular economies.
