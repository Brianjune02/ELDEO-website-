Who we are

Local action for stronger futures.

ELDEO is a community-focused organization working to turn local challenges into practical opportunities. Our work brings together education, mentorship, environmental conservation, women's and youth empowerment, and digital skills.

🎓

Education & Mentorship

Supporting learners through guidance, counselling, life skills, career awareness and pathways back to education.

🌱

Climate & Environment

Promoting practical community solutions including tree planting, rainwater harvesting and circular-economy approaches.

💻

Digital Inclusion

Helping young people, women and communities build practical digital skills for learning, work and enterprise.

What we do

Programs designed around real community needs.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Girls' Life Skills

Education Support

Environmental Action

Featured projects

Turning ideas into measurable community action.

Climate + Education

Harvesting Hope

A rainwater-harvesting and school-garden initiative that linked environmental resilience with student nutrition and school participation. The initiative planted 200 trees and supported food production for students.

Girls + Life Skills

ABSTAIN YOU GAIN CLUB

A life-skills and mentorship initiative launched in 2024 to support girls across schools with guidance, confidence, decision-making and educational continuity.

Women + Youth

ECOGROW Community Hub

A planned community hub bringing together circular-economy skills, environmental action, digital learning and livelihood opportunities for women and youth.

Digital Inclusion

Community ICT Hub

A practical digital-skills space designed to connect communities with computer training, data skills, digital enterprise, content creation and online opportunities.
