Who we are

Local action. Practical solutions. Lasting impact.

ELDEO (Elshaddai Development and Empowerment Organization) is a community-based organization focused on empowering underserved communities through education, mentorship, digital inclusion, youth and women empowerment, environmental conservation and livelihood innovation.

✓
Education & mentorship
Supporting learners with guidance, life skills and opportunities.
✓
Women & youth empowerment
Building practical skills, livelihoods and pathways into the digital economy.
✓
Climate & circular economy
Promoting locally relevant environmental and livelihood solutions.
✓
Digital inclusion
Connecting communities to ICT skills, information and opportunity.
What we do

Programs designed around real community needs

We combine education, technology, environmental action and economic empowerment to address interconnected barriers facing communities.

🎓

Education & Mentorship

Career guidance, life skills, counselling, learner support and initiatives that help children and young people stay connected to education.

💻

Digital Skills & ICT

Community ICT access and training in computer literacy, digital finance, e-commerce, online work, content creation and emerging technologies.

🌱

Climate & Environment

Rainwater harvesting, tree planting, climate-smart practices and circular-economy approaches that connect environmental action with livelihoods.

👩🏾‍🤝‍👩🏽

Women & Youth Empowerment

Practical skills, enterprise pathways and community initiatives that strengthen participation and economic opportunity.

📊

Data for Development

Using responsible data collection, analysis and visualization to improve community programs and support evidence-informed decisions.

🤝

Community Partnerships

Working with schools, local leaders, institutions and development partners to turn ideas into scalable community action.
